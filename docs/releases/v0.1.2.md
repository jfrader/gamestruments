## What Shipped

- **Adventure** recipe (PR #65): eight sections across folk, dark, and
  orchestral styles, with `set_adventure_state`.
- **Racing Extended v2** (PR #67): Ignition, Slipstream, Redline, and Cooldown
  plus a ten-section autoplay tour; Original Racing keeps its six parts and
  stays the native default. The browser Lab defaults to Extended.
- **Godot kit refresh** (PR #68): three independent native examples replace the
  bundled racer; concise buyer docs whose snippets are executed in CI; a
  deterministic free docs+example bundle; packaging and release-packet updates.
- Native Suspense transitions now consume each score's serialized rules, so the
  0.8 progress Disconnect applies to Extended only — matching the browser.
- The Godot player leaves its audio child to Godot's own teardown.
