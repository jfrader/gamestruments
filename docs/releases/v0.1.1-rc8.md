## What Changed Since rc7

- Product repositioned as **Gamestruments — Adaptive Music for Godot 4** (slug
  `gamestruments-godot`); Pocket Circuit is the racing recipe and Night Circuit
  remains the demo (PR #42).
- **Suspense recipe** added to the engine and the shipped Godot addon: song-form
  sections, `original`/`extended` arrangements, `terminal`/`cipher`/`noir`
  styles, `set_trace_state`, and form controls (`cue_section`, `set_form_hold`,
  `advance_form`, `is_form_held`, `get_current_section`) (PR #25). All review
  findings were fixed before merge, including a shared trace-selection table and
  cue re-arm in both runtimes.
- Buyer documentation now covers both recipes: `kit/docs/api.md`, buyer README,
  quickstart, limitations, contract, QA runbook, and listing (PR #43).
- Native libraries rebuilt with the new engine on all three platforms.
