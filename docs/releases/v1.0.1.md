## What Shipped

- **Theme Ride** (GURI-907) rejoins the Suspense phase pool as its 28th phase: a
  Groove at energy 68, 8 bars, carrying the Decrypt solo's high-register cell
  and the Full Breach late hook over the shared arc, with a pinned 4/4 rock
  backbeat (kick 1 and 3, snare 2 and 4, offbeat hats) on every bar plus seeded
  ghost/fill detail. The backbeat is authored in its own stream, so no other
  phase's kit choice changed.
- **Audio Lab Score Debugger** (GURI-908): a new Debugger view with one step grid
  per bar (8th or 16th subdivision inferred from the events), hit counts,
  first/last tick and velocity range per voice, per-voice Solo/Mute that filters
  the events that reach the audio graph, and a Play bar action that holds the
  transport on that bar. The debugger's transient bar slice is excluded from the
  phase list, the section dropdown and the prev/next stepper.

### Consumer impact

Suspense `seeded` and `all-phases` takes change in this release: the pool grew
from 27 to 28 phases, so the composer draws a different form and a take can now
include Theme Ride. A same-seed take is deterministic within 1.0.1 but is not the
song 1.0.0 produced. Racing and Adventure output is unchanged. The debugger is a
Lab-only view and does not affect the runtime API.
