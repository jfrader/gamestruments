## Gameplay and Media

Night Circuit is a playable three-lap assisted-circuit race against one rival.
The player controls throttle/brake, lane and boost; real race state drives music
through `race_music.gd`. There are no manual music-section buttons.

An [exact-archive gameplay recapture](https://uploads.linear.app/a9456217-9d82-43a1-9c1c-b0869f09e484/3ffa54d3-161e-4e61-bce3-042814a08def/190ab113-f8e6-4aef-a6ab-455209e07ff4)

## Human Acceptance Handoff

Verify the archive digest above, extract to a clean directory, open
`kit/demo/project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2, wait for import, and press F5 then Enter.
Use W/S or Up/Down for throttle/brake, A/D or Left/Right for lanes, Space for
boost, Escape for pause, and R to restart. Follow `kit/demo/README.md` and
`docs/kit-qa-runbook.md`; report actual OS/architecture and results, not inferred
