## What Changed Since rc3

- Human acceptance found the demo exposed only one of the four shipped styles.
  The single circuit is now a four-circuit series, one per style, switchable in
  the garage with live regeneration:
  Night Circuit (`neon`, `night-circuit-001`), Harbor Sprint (`funk`,
  `harbor-sprint-002`), Canyon Run (`fusion`, `canyon-run-003`), Micro Mile
  (`chip`, `micro-mile-004`). Each has its own accent color and track
  proportions.
- Steering remains the heading-based grip model from rc3 (PR #31).
- `race_music.gd` exposes `configure(style, seed)`; the race telemetry and six
  sections are unchanged.

## Gameplay and Media

Night Circuit is one of four playable three-lap races against one rival with
heading-based grip-assisted steering. A/D turns the car, releasing the steering
straightens it, and the circuit wall contains it. The garage switches circuits
with A/D or PREV/NEXT and regenerates that circuit's style and seed immediately.
Real race state drives music through `race_music.gd`; there are no manual
music-section buttons.

## Human Acceptance Handoff

Verify the archive digest above, extract to a clean directory, open
`kit/demo/project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2, wait for import, and press F5 then Enter.
In the garage use A/D or PREV/NEXT to hear each circuit's style. Use W/S or
Up/Down for throttle/brake, A/D or Left/Right to steer, Space for boost, Escape
for pause, and R to restart. Follow `kit/demo/README.md` and
