## Gameplay and Media

The demo opens with the intro card, then the garage switches circuits with A/D
or PREV/NEXT and regenerates that circuit's style and seed immediately. Races
are three laps against one rival with heading-based grip-assisted steering;
real race state drives music through `race_music.gd` with no manual
music-section buttons.
