## Why rc2 Was Rejected

Human acceptance on 2026-09-10 found the car strafed sideways between lanes while
staying parallel to the track ("like a crab"); steering did not read as driving.
The rc2 tag and archive remain immutable for provenance but are not launch
candidates. PR #31 replaced the lane-strafe model with heading-based
grip-assisted steering, true ellipse-normal track offsets, and circuit-wall
containment, while keeping the music telemetry and sections unchanged. The
previous `v0.1.0` parameter-panel archive is likewise rejected and unchanged.

## Gameplay and Media

Night Circuit is a playable three-lap assisted-circuit race against one rival
with heading-based grip-assisted steering. A/D turns the car, releasing the
steering straightens it, and the circuit wall contains it. Real race state drives
music through `race_music.gd`; there are no manual music-section buttons.
